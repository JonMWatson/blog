# HEY! Check out this email.

HEY for You is a great product offering many features that blow other email services out of the water. (i.e. The Screener, The Feed, and others) But when Basecamp released HEY for Work they introduced several new features exclusive to the Work version. Among them includes: better collaboration features, custom domain support, email extensions, and many other features.

## What Makes HEY for Work Special?

### Enhanced Collaboration
HEY for Work takes email collaboration to the next level. Teams can share inboxes, collaborate on responses, and maintain transparency in communication in ways that traditional email simply can't match.

### Custom Domain Support
This was a game-changer for businesses. Being able to use HEY's innovative features with your own company domain means you get the best of both worlds - professional branding with cutting-edge email management.

### Email Extensions
The ability to extend HEY's functionality means teams can customize their email experience to match their specific workflows and needs.

### Streamlined Organization
The same organizational features that make HEY for You great - The Screener, The Feed, Paper Trail - are enhanced for team environments, making it easier to manage both personal and collaborative email.

## Why This Matters

Email is still the backbone of business communication, but most email clients haven't evolved much in decades. HEY for Work represents a genuine reimagining of what business email could be.

The features that make personal email management better - like the ability to screen emails, organize them into digestible feeds, and maintain clear paper trails - become even more powerful in a business context where communication clarity and efficiency are paramount.

If you're tired of drowning in email or struggling with email collaboration in your team, HEY for Work might just be the solution you've been looking for.
